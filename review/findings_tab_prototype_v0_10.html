One thing was added: a header naming the source document and the conversion date, above the document’s own front matter rather than in place of it.

How to read the material

What a brief is, and what the two classes on this page do and do not do.

A brief is a short, citable document. Briefs on this page fall into two classes.

Results briefs report modeled tier results from a fixed set of simulations — for the baseline, a theme, a strategy, a sector, or a place. They report what the model produces; they do not predict what will happen, judge whether results are acceptable, or recommend any course of action.

Reference briefs describe the pieces the simulations are built from — what a strategy changes about system operations, or what a hydroclimate model assumes. They are part of the COEQWAL process and contain no model results.
